At your service

Here’s what I do

Brand Strategy

Before changing how you show up, it helps to understand what you want people to see. I dig into your business and the people you need to reach (a lifetime of being nosey as hell has prepared me well for this), helping you find a position you can stand behind. Now all that creative work we will tackle has a clear direction.

Brand identity

You need a logo for your new company. Or you want to refresh (or “glow up,” as the kids like to say) your current one. I do this, along with building out your entire visual world with typography, colors, imagery, and all the details that make a brand feel like itself. I care a lot about your success and want to create something you feel extremely proud of. I know together we’ll get there.

Campaigns + launches

You’re getting ready to launch or attend something, and there’s a lot riding on it. I have a lot of experience developing creative concepts and making all the pieces work together, from the first announcement to the event floor. You’ve put a lot into this. Let’s get people paying attention.

Website design

Your website has a job to do. That doesn’t mean we have to squeeze all the life out of it. I arrived on the glorious World Wide Web in the ’90s and promptly made myself at home. It was weird and full of people making things up as they went along. Today, I design websites that make it easy for people to find what they need, with enough personality to make them want to stick around. Life’s too short to put more mediocre websites into the world.

Presentations

You know your material inside out. Figuring out what belongs on a slide is another matter. I help you figure out what to say and how to show it, so people understand what you’re asking them to get behind. Yes, you should feel proud to put this on a screen and stand beside it, but it should also convince others to fund, support, or buy from you. I’ll make sure your presentation does both.

Toolkits + guidelines

You have a brand you love. Now other people need to use it. I create toolkits and guidelines that take the guesswork out of how things should look, with practical templates your team can make their own. I can also help you choose the right software or platforms and figure out how to keep everything organized and running smoothly. I want this to make everyone’s life easier, including yours!

About me

Great Big Yarn is the independent studio of Melissa Stafford-Woodruff.

You are lucky if you have found a career you love, and I consider myself pretty lucky. Psychologist Adam Mastroianni writes about finding “the job that’s crazy in the same way that you are crazy,” and many years ago, I found mine as a designer. Over the past 20 years, I’ve designed everything from art exhibitions (75+ of them) to books, brands and websites (too many to count).
